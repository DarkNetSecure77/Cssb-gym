About CSSB Gym

More Than A Gym.

CSSB Gym is a dedicated fitness destination in Karawal Nagar, Delhi. We bring together a wide range of fitness activities — from strength and weight training to yoga, dance fitness, and more — under one roof.

Whether your goal is movement, flexibility, strength, or overall well-being, our space is built to support your personal fitness journey. Every session is an opportunity to move better, feel stronger, and stay consistent.
